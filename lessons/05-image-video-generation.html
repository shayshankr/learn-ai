Image & Video Generation

Type a sentence, get a picture. Here's how these tools work, what they're good for, and a style picker to get the idea.

The tools

🎨

Midjourney

Best-in-class artistic quality. Runs through Discord, has a small learning curve.

🖼️

Built into ChatGPT — easiest way to generate an image if you're already chatting.

🎬

Text-to-video — describe a short clip and get moving footage. Still evolving fast.

🎙️

ElevenLabs

Text-to-speech and voice cloning — turns written text into natural-sounding audio.

How it actually works (in one paragraph)

These models were trained on huge numbers of image-caption pairs, so they learned associations between words and visual patterns. When you type a prompt, the model starts from random noise and gradually "denoises" it, step by step, toward something that matches your text — a process called diffusion. It's not retrieving a photo from a database; it's generating pixels from scratch, guided by your words.

⚠️ Worth knowing

These models were trained on huge datasets scraped from the web, which raises real questions about artist consent and copyright — still being actively debated and litigated. Be thoughtful about using AI images commercially, and never generate images of real people without their consent.
