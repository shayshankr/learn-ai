Better Context, Better Output

You know how to prompt. This lesson is about what surrounds the prompt: what you feed it, how you shape what it hands back, and why a long chat quietly gets worse.

Three habits that change everything

Give it real material, not a description of it. Instead of describing a document from memory, paste the actual document in. AI answers about a summary of a thing are worse than answers about the thing itself.

Ask for structured output when you'll reuse the answer. If you're going to copy the result into a spreadsheet, a script, or another tool, ask for a table or JSON instead of a paragraph. It costs nothing to ask and saves you the reformatting.

Start a new chat for a new task. Every message you send re-sends the whole conversation so far. A long thread doesn't just get slower, older details get harder for the model to weigh against everything said since. A fresh chat with the relevant context pasted back in often outperforms a 40-message thread.

Same question, two shapes of answer

Toggle between asking for prose and asking for structure. Same underlying information, very different usefulness depending on what you're about to do with it.

📝 Prose 📊 Structured

Prompt: "Here are my March expenses: rent 1200, groceries 340, transport 90, one subscription refund of -15. Summarize this."

In March, your largest expense was rent at 1200, followed by groceries at 340 and transport at 90. You also received a small refund of 15 from a subscription, bringing your total spend to around 1615.
