No-Code AI Builders

You don't need to know how to code to build something useful with AI anymore. These tools turn plain descriptions into working automations and apps.

Two flavors

🔗

Automation: Zapier, n8n, Make

  • Connect apps together: "when X happens, do Y"
  • e.g. new email → summarize with AI → post to Slack
🏗️

App builders: Bolt, Lovable, v0, Replit

  • Describe an app in a sentence, get a working prototype
  • e.g. "a simple expense tracker with charts"

Why this matters for beginners

💡 The real shift

Five years ago, "build me an app" meant hiring a developer or spending months learning to code. Today a rough working version can exist in minutes. It won't replace real engineering for anything serious — but for personal tools, prototypes, and small automations, it's a genuinely new capability worth trying.

🎯 Try it for real

Think of one small, repetitive thing you do by hand — sorting emails, logging expenses, reminding people about something. Try describing it to a no-code builder and see how close it gets on the first try.
