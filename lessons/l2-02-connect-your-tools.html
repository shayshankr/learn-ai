Connecting AI to Your Real Tools

Copy-pasting into a chat window is fine, but it's not the ceiling. AI that's actually connected to your calendar, inbox, or docs can check things instead of being told them.

What "connected" actually means

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets an AI plug into an app, like Gmail, a calendar, GitHub, or Slack, so it can read from or act on your real data instead of only the text you paste in. Claude connected to your calendar can actually check whether you're free Thursday. Claude without it can only work from what you type.

⚠️ Start read-only

The first time you connect a new tool, give it permission to read before you give it permission to write or delete. Review what it's about to send, post, or remove before you approve it, especially the first few times. Most good integrations ask before anything irreversible; be wary of any that don't.
