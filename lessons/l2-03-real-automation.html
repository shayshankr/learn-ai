A Real Automation, End to End

This is Lesson 9 for people who don't want to write code. Same idea: pick something you actually do by hand, and turn it into an automation you can turn on this week.

Pick a real one

Click one to see the brief and a starter description you can paste into Zapier, n8n, or Make's AI step.

Auto-sort my inbox

A new email arrives, an AI step reads it and decides which folder or label it belongs in, so your inbox sorts itself instead of you doing it every morning.

📋 Starter description

When a new email arrives, read the subject and first paragraph, decide if it's Urgent, Newsletter, or Needs Reply, and apply the matching label. If unsure, leave it in the inbox untouched.

The shape, recapped

Trigger, then an AI step to make a judgment call, then an action. That's it, that's the whole pattern from Lesson 6, just pointed at something real.

⚡

Trigger

The event that starts it: new email, new form response, a scheduled time.

🤖

AI step

The judgment call a fixed rule can't make well: is this urgent? What's the summary?

✅

Action

What actually happens: a label applied, a message sent, a row added.

🎯 Try it for real

Give yourself 60 minutes this week. Pick one idea above, build it in Zapier, n8n, or Make's free tier, and actually turn it on.
