Chatbots 101

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot Chat — they all look like a text box. Here's what's actually different between them, and how to try one out.

Meet the big three (plus one)

These are general-purpose AI assistants: you type a question or task in plain English, and they respond in plain English. No installation, no coding.

💬

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

The one most people have heard of. Huge plugin/app ecosystem, strong at general tasks and image generation built in.

🧠

Claude (Anthropic)

Known for careful, well-reasoned writing and strong coding help. Good default if your friend will use AI for writing or analysis.

✨

Gemini (Google)

Deeply integrated with Google Search, Docs, and Gmail — handy if your friend already lives in Google's ecosystem.

🧑‍💻

Copilot (Microsoft)

Built into Windows and Office. Good starting point for someone who's never touched an AI tool before.

💡 Which one should a beginner pick?

Honestly, any of them. They're all free to start, and the skills transfer between them almost completely. Pick whichever is easiest to sign up for and just start typing.

What chatbots are great at — and what they're not

✅ Good at

  • Explaining concepts in different ways until it clicks
  • First drafts — emails, messages, outlines
  • Brainstorming and rubber-ducking ideas
  • Summarizing long text you paste in

⚠️ Be careful with

  • Very recent events (knowledge has a cutoff date)
  • Exact facts, stats, citations — it can sound confident and still be wrong
  • Anything you paste that's private or sensitive
  • Math done purely "in its head" without a tool

We'll go deeper on the "sounding confident but wrong" problem — called hallucination — in Lesson 8.
